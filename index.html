How a call gets made

  1. Scan

    Pull Solana's trending pools over the last hour and day, skipping stablecoins and big non-meme tokens.

  2. Check the valve

    Mint and freeze authority, pool depth and pool age. If the dev can still print or freeze, the valve shuts.

  3. Read four gauges

    Pressure, flow, safety and heat each score the coin 0 to 100 and read green, amber or red.

  4. Make the call

    Three greens make a Buy. A red safety valve makes an Avoid. Anything mixed is a Watch.
